# OBS Settings

---

## OBS `Settings -> Stream` tab

* Multitrack Video: ✓ Enable Enhanced Broadcasting

---

### Recording settings:

**Everything default except:**

* Video encoder: AV1 (if you don't have AV1, choose H.265/HEVC)
* Rate control: Variable Bitrate with Target

---

## OBS `Settings -> Video` tab

### Video settings:

* Base (Canvas) Resolution: whatever resolution your monitor is  
* Output (Canvas) Resolution: what you want the output to be. Some people downscale from 4k/1440p to 1080p. If downscaling, turn on Lanczos below in Downscale Filter.
* Downscale Filter: Lanczos
* Common FPS Values: 60
